Privacy Policy

App: Georgian Citizenship Trainer (ge.citizenship.trainer.plus)

Effective: 22 August 2026, revised 17 September 2026

Developer: a private individual, contact — zurabgedenidze90@gmail.com

In short

The app works fully offline. It does not collect, transmit or store outside your device any personal data. There are no accounts, ads or analytics.

What data is processed

Everything listed below is stored only on your device and is never sent anywhere:

  • your study progress: which questions you have seen, what you answered correctly and incorrectly, when the next review is due;
  • bookmarks and your notes on questions;
  • mock exam results and your streak of study days;
  • settings: interface and question languages, theme, daily goal, exam date and the time of the daily reminder, if you set them.

This data lives in the app's protected folder. If backup is enabled in Android system settings, it becomes part of your personal Google backup — this is done by the operating system; the developer has no access to it.

What data is NOT processed

The app does not request or receive: name, email, phone number, contacts, location, camera, microphone, files, advertising identifiers, browsing history.

Permissions

  • Internet — the permission is declared by the app's technical platform but is not actually used: all 600 questions and the fonts are part of the app, and it makes no network requests.
  • Notifications — requested only if you turn on the daily reminder in settings yourself. Reminders are created and shown on the device itself, without a server or the internet; you can turn them off in the app or in system settings.
  • Speech synthesis — the app uses the voice engine installed in the system to read a question aloud in Georgian. The text is passed to the Android system component on your device; whether data is sent to the internet is decided by the voice engine you chose, under its own rules.

Emails to the author

All emails to the author from the app open your mail client with pre-filled text; the email is sent only if you send it yourself, and the text is visible and editable before sending. What is pre-filled:

  • "Write to the author" (settings) and "Answer three questions" ("My path", after passing) — app version, platform, current settings and brief usage statistics (number of answers, sessions and exams, streak, number of notes, the last app error, if any);
  • "Report a problem with this question" — the question text, the answer per the key and the app version.

The emails contain no device identifiers, accounts or precise data about you.

When recruiting testers for closed testing on Google Play, the developer processes participants' Google account addresses outside the app, only to grant access to the test, and deletes them once it ends.

Web version

The web version runs in the browser: progress is stored in the browser's local storage on your device and is not sent anywhere. After the first load it works offline. Pages are served by static hosting (GitHub Pages); like any web server, it sees the IP address and technical request headers — the developer has no access to these logs, and no analytics or third-party scripts are used.

Store rating

After your first passed mock exam the app may show the system rating dialog of Google Play or the App Store. The dialog belongs to the store; the app transmits nothing and does not learn whether you left a rating.

Sharing with third parties

None. Data does not leave the device.

Children

The app is intended for adults preparing for the citizenship exam and is not directed at children under 13.

How to delete data

You can delete your progress completely in two ways:

  1. in the app: Settings → Data → Reset everything;
  2. by uninstalling the app, or via Android system settings: Apps → Citizenship GE → Storage → Clear data. In the web version — by clearing the site data in your browser.

Before deleting, you can save your progress: Settings → Data → Export to a file.

Changes to this policy

If the app gains features that work over the network, this policy will be updated before such a version is released, and the data safety section in the stores will be brought in line.

Contact

Questions about data processing: zurabgedenidze90@gmail.com (the same "Write to the author" button is in the app settings).
