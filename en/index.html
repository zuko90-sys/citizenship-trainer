Georgian citizenship exam — prepare without rote learning

600 questions from the open bank of the Public Service Development Agency: history, Georgian language, basics of law. A realistic mock exam, spaced repetition and explanations — free, offline, no ads.

What is inside

  • A realistic examThree blocks of 10 questions, 20 minutes per block, pass mark 7 of 10 in each. “Like the real one” mode is Georgian only.
  • Spaced repetitionMiss a question and it returns tomorrow; answer confidently and it returns later. Problem questions come first.
  • See if you are readyA forecast per subject and the chance to pass all blocks; the plan adapts to your exam date.
  • ExplanationsShort explanations with a reference to the article of the Constitution or historical context.
  • A little every dayStreak with freezes, exam of the day, a one-minute quick round and a challenge-a-friend code.
  • My pathA step-by-step guide to the procedure with sources: sda.gov.ge and decree No. 996. A separate mode for restoring citizenship.

Web version

Runs in the browser with no install and works offline after the first load. Progress is stored only in your browser. There are no notifications on the web, and speech depends on the voices your browser has.

This is an unofficial app: it is not affiliated with the Public Service Development Agency of Georgia and does not represent any government body. The question bank is published by the agency; the source is sda.gov.ge.
